Bill Panda is a recurring bill tracker. You add your bills, you mark them
paid, you see what's coming up. That's the whole product.

It doesn't ask for an account. It doesn't show ads. It doesn't try to sell
you a budgeting course. It syncs across your devices over iCloud and has a
home screen widget. It's free.

I built it because I wanted this app to exist and the existing options
were all trying to be something else.
