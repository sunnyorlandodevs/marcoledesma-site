Most of my writing is technical and most of it is aimed at the part of
software development that doesn't get the spotlight: the legacy codebase,
the integration that almost works, the meeting that should have been a
document.

The brief is the same across everything I write: assume the reader is
smart, assume they're busy, don't waste either. If a sentence isn't
doing work, cut it.

Currently most of my published writing lives on
[dev.to](https://dev.to/m4rcoperuano), with the canonical versions slowly
migrating here.
